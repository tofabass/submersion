Run Lite or Full?

Full is every effect at full quality. Lite caps resolution, frame rate, particles, glow, trails and the glass backdrop so it stays smooth on phones and older laptops. Saved settings work in both, and you can switch any time in the Theme menu (T).

Recommended for Full
  • A discrete GPU, or recent integrated graphics (Intel Iris Xe, AMD Radeon 680M, Apple M1 or newer)
  • 8 GB RAM or more
  • Current Chrome, Edge or Firefox with hardware acceleration on
