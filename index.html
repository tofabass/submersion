Background

Pick or drop several PNG or SVG files at once: each one gets its own icon layer, up to 8 icon layers. White-on-transparent icons take any colour with Recolour.

Colour comes from Recolour below. Shape, sides, points and stroke width redraw the shape, so oscillators and audio cannot drive them; everything else maps as usual.

Particles live in the image's space, so this layer's Symmetry folds them into the kaleidoscope like any other layer. Emitter symmetry copies the particles themselves around the emitter center. Flow and turbulence speeds are whole numbers per loop, so exports stay seamless.

Random pulse timing

Oscillators

Off by default. When ticked, each roll gives every oscillator a new wave shape and rate and maps one of them to one of the ticked effects. Undo roll takes it all back.

Layer colours

Off by default. When ticked, each roll also recolours the layers ticked in the Layers panel with a random colour scheme. Undo roll takes it back.

Roll changes the ticked effects on the ticked targets. Random amount caps how far each one can move from where it is now; at 100% anything goes.

Mappings
Selected control:

A preset adds ready-made mappings, gates and smoothing. Built-in presets never change mappings you add yourself. Save preset keeps every mapping you have now, and picking a different preset replaces them.

Two sources on one slider add together, then stop at the slider's limits. A band mapping can follow Low, Mid and High together; it uses their average. Mappings save with Look presets and projects.

    Run Lite or Full?

    Full is every effect at full quality. Lite caps resolution, frame rate, particles, glow, trails and the glass backdrop so it stays smooth on phones and older laptops. Saved settings work in both, and you can switch any time in Settings (T).

    Recommended for Full
    • A discrete GPU, or recent integrated graphics (Intel Iris Xe, AMD Radeon 680M, Apple M1 or newer)
    • 8 GB RAM or more
    • Current Chrome, Edge or Firefox with hardware acceleration on
